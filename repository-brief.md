Amphetamine is a tray app that keeps your computer awake on macOS and Windows. It runs on Electron 43 and TypeScript, uses `powerSaveBlocker` for sleep prevention, and supports timed or indefinite sessions with a battery-based auto-off. OS differences go through thin main-process platform adapters. The build pipeline (Rslib for main/preload, Rsbuild for renderer) outputs CJS from ESM source; packaging produces macOS DMG/ZIP and Windows NSIS/portable installers with Electron fuses flipped post-build.
